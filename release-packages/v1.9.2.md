## Revised v1.9.2

This replaces the original v1.9.2 package with organized Settings and interactive menu gestures.

**Already running v1.9.2? Download and reinstall the revised full ZIP below.** The original updater compares version numbers and will not offer another build with the same number. Preferences, lyric timing and listening history keep their existing keys. Versions 1.9.0–1.9.1 can update through the feed as usual.

## Changes

- **Eight Settings submenus:** Text & accessibility, Appearance & glass, Now Playing, Lyrics, Home & clock, Touch & keyboard, Updates, and History & help. Related options stay together; only one group is shown at a time. The artwork-size shortcut opens the correct group directly.
- **Dynamic swipe Back:** swipe left to right inside Settings, away from text fields and sliders. The category list follows the drag; release past **80 px** to confirm. Back at the category hub closes Settings.
- **Dynamic swipe to close:** pull the header down, or pull down on content already at its top, from any submenu. Release past **72 px** to close the whole menu. Short, reversed or cancelled pulls return to their starting state. Reduce Motion keeps the controls and thresholds without movement.
- The **⋯** lyric menu replaces Timing/Reload buttons. Choose Timing… or Reload lyrics; Earlier, Later, Reset and Match a line remain inside Timing.
- Removed **LIVE LYRICS** and the tap-to-seek hint. Tapping a synced lyric still seeks to that line.
- The official update feed is prefilled. Custom URLs, disabled checks and other preferences are preserved.

## Full installation for new users or the original v1.9.2

Download **LiquidTouch-v1.9.2.zip** below and extract the whole archive. It includes the complete theme, extension, installers, preview and source; no previous LiquidTouch installation is required.

Install Spotify and a compatible Spicetify version, then follow the included README. From PowerShell in the extracted **LiquidTouch** folder:

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Install-Windows.ps1
```

Close Spotify when instructed. The official feed is already filled in this revised package:

```text
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OrbitalShift4/Spotify-LiquidTouch/main/latest.json
```

From v1.9.0–1.9.1, use Settings → Updates → Check now, enter the feed once if needed, then approve Update. The first launch after updating shows patch notes. A normally reinstalled v1.9.2 can also show these notes through Settings → Updates → What’s new.
